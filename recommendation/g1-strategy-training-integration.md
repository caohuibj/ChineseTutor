# G1 integration with F1 TrainingMove recommendation

Status: **normative draft**

Mother Strategies become first-class learner targets. F1 may recommend training a Strategy itself when the Profile shows a procedural/automation gap that is not explained by underlying Knowledge/Ability failure.

---

## 1. When Strategy becomes the primary target

Typical cases:

### Mastery gap
The learner knows relevant facts/abilities but cannot organize the procedure even after it is named.

→ `establish` / `scaffolded_practice` on the Strategy.

### Automation gap
The learner executes correctly after an H2 method reminder but does not self-trigger.

→ `fade_scaffold` / `automation`.

### Transfer gap
The learner uses the Strategy in one familiar TaskType but not another TaskType with the same procedural structure.

→ `transfer_probe` with a different TaskType/material while holding unnecessary complexity stable.

---

## 2. When Strategy should NOT be the primary target

Do not target Strategy merely because the final answer is wrong.

Examples:

- 文言翻译失败 because keyword meaning is wrong → target lexical Knowledge/Ability first, not S7;
- 人物题 has no evidence because text location failed → target R/location, not S1/S8;
- argument paragraph has good reasoning but fragmented wording → target E/expression conversion, not S5 reteaching.

F2 causal diagnosis remains authoritative inside a Question.

---

## 3. Question selection for Strategy training

A suitable Question should:

- naturally require the mother Strategy;
- have prerequisites within the learner's current readiness;
- make Strategy invocation/execution observable;
- avoid excessive unrelated complexity;
- use a new TaskType/material when transfer is the goal.

Strategy training should not rely on a Question whose solution can bypass the target procedure so completely that Strategy evidence is unobservable.

---

## 4. Typical move shapes

### Establish S1

```text
Task:人物/主旨 short answer
Hint: H2/H3
Goal: execute evidence→explanation→conclusion correctly
```

### Fade S1

```text
Same semantic demand
H2 → H0/H1
Goal: self-trigger middle explanation step
```

### Transfer S2

```text
previously used in modern-text comparison
next: poetry or classical comparison
H0
Goal: independently create shared comparison dimension
```

### Automate S7

```text
new classical sentence
no “五步法” reminder
moderate time constraint
Goal: self-initiate keyword/syntax/ellipsis checking
```

---

## 5. Strategy saturation

Once a Strategy has adequate M2 routine evidence, more same-TaskType repetitions lose value.

F1 should shift toward:

- cue fading;
- TaskType variation;
- unfamiliar transfer;
- timed automation;
- retention review when due.

Do not reward “method worksheet volume”.

---

## 6. Invariants

1. Strategy is targeted only when procedural evidence indicates a procedural gap.
2. Underlying prerequisite failure outranks Strategy practice when it blocks observation.
3. Strategy automation is trained by cue removal/self-trigger, not merely harder questions.
4. Transfer changes context/TaskType while preserving the procedural construct.
5. Same-form repetition is suppressed after the evidence purpose is satisfied.

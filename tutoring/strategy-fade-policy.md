# G1 Strategy prompting and fade policy

Status: **normative draft**

Mother Strategies are only useful if they can move from explicit support to autonomous invocation. This document connects G1 Strategy nodes to C1/C2/F1/F2.

---

## 1. Two different things must be observed

### Strategy Mastery
Can the learner execute the procedure correctly once it is active?

### Strategy Automation
Does the learner recognize when to invoke it and run it without being told?

These can diverge.

Example:

```text
S1 证据—解释—结论
Mastery M2
Automation A1
```

Meaning:

> 一提醒“把证据为什么支持结论补出来”，学生就能正确做；但看到新题时还不会自己启动。

This is a high-value fade-scaffold target, not a need to reteach the Strategy from scratch.

---

## 2. Support ladder for Strategy learning

Typical progression:

```text
explicit principle + worked example
→ H3 guide through one missing operation
→ H2 name/recall the mother Strategy
→ H1 task orientation only
→ H0 self-trigger
→ H0 under time/attention pressure
→ H0 in unfamiliar TaskType/material transfer
```

This is not a mandatory chronological ladder; prior evidence may place the learner higher immediately.

---

## 3. H2 evidence semantics

F2 H2 may explicitly name or cue a Strategy.

Example:

> “这题先用‘证据—解释—结论’检查一下。”

A successful response can support:

- Strategy execution/mastery;
- downstream Ability execution if the learner still produces it.

But it does **not** independently prove:

- Strategy A2/A3 self-trigger.

C2 should record the Strategy node as guided/partial for automation purposes.

---

## 4. H3 and Strategy learning

A key guiding question may expose one missing Strategy step without naming the whole model.

Example S8:

> “为什么天子亲自来劳军，会让周亚夫这个选择更能说明他的品质？”

This can provide evidence that the learner can complete the “意义” step after a targeted cue, but not that S8 is fully automatic.

---

## 5. Fade obligation

When a learner succeeds after Strategy support and the TrainingMove targets independence/automation, F1/F2 should create a comparable follow-up where:

- semantic demand is held roughly stable;
- the Strategy cue is reduced or removed;
- the material/Question is sufficiently different to avoid simple recall.

Do not raise difficulty and remove support simultaneously unless the move explicitly targets both.

---

## 6. Strategy transfer evidence

A Strategy may reach M3 only when the learner adapts it across varied contexts without rigidly forcing irrelevant steps.

Examples:

### S1
Successful use in modern人物、文言人物、诗歌情感 does not require identical sentence output, but the evidence→warrant logic remains visible.

### S2
Successful comparison across诗歌 and信息文本 shows procedural transfer if the learner independently establishes a valid shared dimension.

### S12
Applying minimal-revision logic to病句 and作文局部修改 may support transfer, but only if higher-level problems are not incorrectly treated as sentence-level edits.

Rigid template use in an inappropriate TaskType is negative evidence for flexible Strategy mastery, even if the wording looks familiar.

---

## 7. Strategy vs output normalization

After the reasoning is complete, F2 may help convert it into exam prose.

Example:

```text
Strategy execution complete:
事实 → 特殊情境 → 差异 → 品质意义

then normalization:
“在……的情况下，周亚夫仍……，与……形成对比，因此更能表现……”
```

The sentence skeleton is not evidence that the Strategy self-triggered.

---

## 8. Mother Strategy review/retention

F3 should usually classify Strategy nodes as `strategy` retention family.

- mastery may remain current while automation becomes due earlier;
- ordinary authentic work can refresh Strategy only when invocation/execution is actually observed;
- passive teacher use of the Strategy does not refresh learner automation.

---

## 9. Invariants

1. Strategy Mastery and Automation remain separate axes.
2. H2 can support mastery but normally weakens self-trigger evidence.
3. Strategy automation is tested by removing the Strategy cue, not by merely increasing question difficulty.
4. Transfer requires flexible adaptation, not mechanical template repetition.
5. Output skeletons do not count as Strategy invocation.
6. Supported success normally creates a later fade obligation when independence is the goal.
